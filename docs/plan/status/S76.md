| S76-01 | S76 | ✅ | #1826 | A document's outline, read where the agent reads ([ADR 0051](../../adr/0051-knowledge-bases.md) decisions 28-29, after STAIR, arXiv 2609.03874, whose fine-tuned retrieval is rejected there). `choregos_core.outline`: ATX headings, fence-aware, numbered by nesting, each section with its line and size and holding its subsections; `find` by number, heading path or unique title. A `files` base's `index.md` lists each document's outline (3 levels, 40 sections, the rest counted) with the line each starts on, beside its `okf_version` (ADR 0174), and the prompt says to read it first. A document's OKF header is not text: a `# comment` in it is no heading, and the lines stay the file's. **What this proves**: `packages/core/tests/test_outline.py` (numbering, fences, ranges, lookup, an ambiguous title not guessed); `packages/runner/tests/test_knowledge.py` (the outline and its lines in `index.md`, the sections not listed counted, a document's OKF header skipped with its lines kept) — red without the change. Finding [#1825](https://github.com/graalsystems/tranibor/issues/1825), fixed here: the `brief` and `deliver` role prompts still sent agents to `INDEX.md`, which ADR 0174 renamed `index.md`; `tests/docs/test_knowledge_index_name.py` (every shipped prompt mentioning `.choregos/knowledge` names the runner's `INDEX`) is red on the previous `main`. **What this does not prove**: that an agent answers better with it; nothing is measured on a real corpus. |
| S76-02 | S76 | ✅ | #1826 | `knowledge_get` reads by section, and a PDF's bookmarks become its headings ([ADR 0051](../../adr/0051-knowledge-bases.md) decisions 30-31, amends [ADR 0138](../../adr/0138-the-knowledge-an-assistant-offers.md) point 4). A conversation's `knowledge_get` takes an optional `section` (number or heading path, a file name in front ignored); a text past its 20 000 characters ends with its outline; an unknown section answers the outline. A PDF's bookmarks (pdfminer, already under MarkItDown) are placed on the lines that say them, in order, possibly after a section number or over two lines, by an indexed lookup linear in the text; one not found invents nothing and is not a warning. **What this proves**: `apps/api/tests/test_conversation_knowledge.py` (a long document cut, then read by number and by a search hit's path; an unknown section), `apps/api/tests/test_knowledge_pdf_outline.py` (a PDF with bookmarks converted with headings and its chunks given heading paths; without bookmarks unchanged; 2 000 missing bookmarks cost 2 000 lookups, never a pass over the text: counted, not timed, and a quadratic mutant is red). **What this does not prove**: real PDFs from the field (the fixtures are hand-written PDFs); PDFs converted before keep their text until converted again. Runs of an `index` base read by section since S76-04. |
| S76-03 | S76 | ✅ | #1826 | The eval measures the first hit and the section ([ADR 0051](../../adr/0051-knowledge-bases.md) decision 32). **Contract change** (`contract-change`, additive): `KnowledgeEvalCase.section`; `eval_result` gains `hit_at_1`, `section_recall` and, per case, `first`, `section`, `section_found`.

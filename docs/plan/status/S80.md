| S80-06 | S80 | 🟡 | — | **Temporal over TLS, payloads encrypted** (plan workstream S4, phase 1; [ADR 0183](../../adr/0183-temporal-over-tls-payloads-encrypted.md)). Every process connected with its own `Client.connect`, in clear, with the default converter (`worker.py`, three times in `train_client.py`, the API's `RealTemporal`, `tools/replay_record.py`): ticket text was readable in the histories.

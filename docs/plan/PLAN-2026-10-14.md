## 2. Challenging the "LLM loops over products" approach

| # | Flaw | Consequence | What S38 does |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | The model knows the past | A 2015–2025 backtest is contaminated: it "guesses" the winners | Closed strategy grammar; the strategist reads an anonymised in-sample profile; the verdict uses the out-of-sample period only, which must start after the model's training cut-off |
| 2 | Multiple testing (products × strategies × parameters) | The best Sharpe is noise | Trials counted; Deflated Sharpe Ratio; strategies capped; no second round on the same out-of-sample period (a failed verdict ends the campaign) |
| 3 | The model computes or copies numbers | Hallucinated figures | A job computes the metrics, the platform marks them measured; `numbers_measured` refuses a memo quoting a number the backtest did not compute |
| 4 | Free data | Terms of use, survivorship bias, adjustments | Several free sources with a composite type and a cross-check; the snapshot is hashed and stored, never re-downloaded on replay. Internal use only |
| 5 | No costs | Profitable on paper only | `cost_bps` per change of position in the engine; no spread, tax or currency model (said in the README) |
| 6 | Regulation (investment advice) | Legal risk if distributed | The output is a research note, never an order; a person decides, attested; disclaimer in the README and the memo skill |
| 7 | One model call per product | Cost grows with the universe | Models are called per campaign (strategies, memo); the loop over products is in the deterministic engine |

## 3. The workflow — `templates/investment-research/`

One work item = one campaign. `snapshot` (job) → `profile` (job) → `strategize` (agent,
`output_schema`) → `backtest` (job) → `verdict` (platform, `evidence_facts` on measured facts,
`relevant_count ≥ 1`, else `not_relevant`) → `memo` (agent, `numbers_measured`) → `decide` (owner,
attested task). Policy `preset:regulated`: no agent and no job reaches the internet; the snapshot job
calls the organisation's `marketdata` connector through the internal API with its run token.

## 5. Changes from the first draft

- Data: several free sources with a cross-check, not a licensed provider (Vincent, 2026-10-14).
- The last step is `decide` by the owner, not a committee: internal use.
- S38-04: the snapshot is a job calling the connector through the internal API, not a system effect
  — one mechanism (jobs) instead of two, and the job's run token is scoped to its run.
- No `parquet` type: CSV is enough for daily bars and keeps the engine on the standard library.

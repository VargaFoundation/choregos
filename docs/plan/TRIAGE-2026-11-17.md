# Triage of the partial stories (2026-11-17)

Every story marked 🟡 (partially delivered) in the status files (`docs/plan/status/<stream>.md`, indexed by `docs/plan/STATUS.md`) on 2026-11-17, sorted into three
piles by [ADR 0180](../adr/0180-one-quarter-of-depth-the-scope-is-frozen.md), each with the reason
read from its STATUS note and the backlog. The workstreams named are those of the roadmap,
[PLAN-2026-11-17-engine-to-product](PLAN-2026-11-17-engine-to-product.md).

`tests/docs/test_partial_triage.py` fails when a 🟡 story of the status files has no row here, or when a row's
pile is not one of the three. A story that turns ✅ may keep its row; a story that turns 🟡 after
2026-11-17 gets a row in the PR that marks it.

**The piles.**

- **finish (W)**: the missing part is closed by workstream W of the roadmap. `depth` is a gap closed
  in the quarter outside any named workstream (a missing test, a guarantee the platform states but
  does not hold). `O3` is a stream ADR 0180 lets finish. `A6` is the documentation purge: the note
  states no gap, or a gap that later work seems to have closed, and the purge re-reads it and either
  marks the story ✅ or names what is left.
- **frozen**: the missing part is not on the roadmap's path; the story stays 🟡 with its note, and
  nothing is done on it until ADR 0180 is amended. Console gaps (a page proved against the mock API
  only, a missing hand check) are frozen until the user tests of workstream A2 say which screens stay.
- **abandon**: the remainder is no longer a deliverable: superseded by a later record or story, a
  description of intended behaviour, a gesture outside this repository, or a gap the CI has closed
  since (the whole API suite runs on PostgreSQL in CI, S42-01).

When unsure, the pile is **frozen**.
